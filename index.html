Insurance Fraud Framework

An ATT&CK-style model of how insurance fraud actually unfolds — extending MITRE’s Fight Fraud Framework with the staged, induced and paper-based techniques that never touch a server log.

How to read this. Each column is a tactic — the actor’s goal at that stage of the fraud lifecycle. F3 models cyber-enabled fraud and stops at the edge of the digital channel. Insurance fraud frequently has no digital channel at all: a staged collision, an inflated garage estimate, a ghost-broked policy. Two tactics here have no F3 equivalent — IFA001 Policy Onboarding (fraud baked in at underwriting) and IFA002 Loss Engineering (manufacturing the insured event itself). Techniques carrying F#### or T#### IDs are reused directly from F3 / ATT&CK where the behaviour is identical; IF#### IDs are new. Illustrative and non-exhaustive — a strawman to start the conversation, not an official matrix.
