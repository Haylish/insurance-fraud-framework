Insurance Fraud Framework

An ATT&CK-style model of how insurance fraud actually unfolds, extending MITRE’s Fight Fraud Framework with the staged, induced and paper-based techniques that never touch a server log.

How to read this. Each column is a tactic, the actor’s goal at that stage of the fraud lifecycle. F3 models cyber-enabled fraud and stops at the edge of the digital channel. Insurance fraud frequently has no digital channel at all: a staged collision, an inflated garage estimate, a ghost-broked policy. Two tactics here are conceptually novel with no F3 equivalent: IFA001 Policy Onboarding (fraud baked in at underwriting) and IFA002 Loss Engineering (manufacturing the insured event itself). Two more, Claim Submission and Claim Maximisation, are insurance-specific breakdowns of F3’s Execution tactic. Techniques carrying F#### or T#### IDs are reused directly from F3 where the behaviour is identical, T#### where F3 itself kept a technique’s native ATT&CK ID rather than reissuing it; IF#### IDs are new. Illustrative and non-exhaustive, a strawman to start the conversation, not an official matrix.
